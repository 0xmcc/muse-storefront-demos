Virtual Try-On

Virtual Try-On is a private fitting room that travels with you. Upload a single photograph and see yourself wearing any piece from Barcelino's curated collection — the exact jacket, in the exact cloth, rendered with the drape and proportion of a garment actually on your shoulders. Browse the collection, compare pieces side by side, save the looks you return to, and order the ones you want. Every look is created for you and for no one else.

There is a moment every customer knows. A jacket looks extraordinary on the rail, and quite different in the mirror. The shoulder sits a fraction wide. The cloth reads warmer under the boutique's light than it will on a Tuesday morning. For a hundred years the only remedy was to come in, take off your coat, and try it on. Virtual Try-On is that fitting room, brought to you.

The service works from a single photograph. Barcelino's atelier photographs every garment in the collection — the weave, the roll of the lapel, the exact hand of a silk-linen — and Virtual Try-On places that specific piece on you, keeping your face, your hair and your build unchanged. What comes back is not a cut-out or an overlay. It is a photograph of you, in that jacket, lit as our campaigns are lit. You see the garment as it will actually look, before anything is ordered.

Your photograph belongs to you. It is used to prepare your fitting and for nothing else — never stored, never sold, never shown to anyone. The whole point of a fitting room is that it is private, and this one is no different.

Maybe You're Wondering…

How accurate is Virtual Try-On?
It is accurate about the things you can see: colour, cloth, cut, lapel width, length and how a piece sits on your frame. It is a very good indication of how a garment will look on you, and it is not a substitute for a tailor's tape.
Will the garment fit exactly the same in person?
Fit depends on your measurements and on the alterations our tailors make. Use Virtual Try-On to decide what suits you, then let the fitting room and our tailors settle the last inch.
Can I try more than one jacket?
As many as you like. Move through the collection and each piece is fitted to the same photograph, so you are comparing garments rather than lighting or pose.
Can I compare different colours?
Yes. Colourways sit beside one another in the collection rail, and a look you have already seen reappears immediately, so you can move back and forth between two of them.
What happens to my photograph?
It is used to prepare your fitting and then discarded. It is never stored, never used for anything else, and never shown to anyone.
Can I save the looks I like?
Save any look to return to it, share it with whoever you take shopping advice from, or order the piece directly from the boutique.
